Four distinct framework terms

Node: named person, setting, activity, signal, interaction area, or concept. Network Edge: a relation between nodes, shown as a line. Edge Bundle: a named set of related network edges, listed in the JSON. Adaptive Edge: a conceptual boundary of adaptation or exploration; it is not directly measured or scored in this snapshot.

Solid lines indicate reported events or direct context; dashed lines are hypotheses or future actions; dotted lines are descriptive/context-only links. The JSON stores relation type, strength/status, evidence, context, and certainty per record. Force layout distances and widths are visual only.
